# 🚀 Space Mission Data Analysis Project

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring a dataset of **500 space missions** spanning from 2025 to 2034. This project tracks launch vehicles, destination targets, mission costs, and success rates to extract actionable aerospace insights.

## 📊 Core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Missions Analyzed:** 500 [1]
* **Average Mission Success Rate:** 92.62% [1]
* **Highest Mission Success:** 100% [1]
* **Total Space Exploration Investment:** ~$138.65 Billion USD [1]
* **Average Fuel Consumption:** 2,543.52 tons per mission [1]
* **Average Payload Weight:** 50.36 tons [1]

## 📈 Key Insights & Summaries

### 1. Mission Distribution by Year
The project shows a consistent launch cadence of roughly 52–53 missions per year, proving steady industry growth:
* **2025 – 2033:** Steady average of ~52 launches annually.
* **2034:** 30 launches (Current partial year dataset).

### 2. Success Rate by Mission Type
Aerospace mining operations lead the industry in reliability, closely followed by exploration:
* **Mining:** 93.51% Average Success [1]
* **Exploration:** 92.89% Average Success [1]
* **Colonization:** 92.53% Average Success [1]
* **Research:** 91.65% Average Success [1]

### 3. Financial & Resource Allocation
* **Highest Average Cost:** Exploration and Mining operations require the heaviest investments (~$278B and ~$296B respectively).
* **Resource Heaviest:** Colonization missions require the highest average fuel consumption (~2,760 tons) due to long-term transit requirements.
